DualShot is a proof-of-concept app for simultaneous front and back camera capture. It uses raw Camera2 concurrent streaming to capture from both cameras at the same time, compositing the front-facing camera as a draggable, resizable picture-in-picture over the back camera view.

The composited scene is saved into photos and baked into recorded videos. The PIP can be moved and resized live during recording.

Currently verified working on Fairphone 5 (Android 15). This is a debug build from the development branch.
